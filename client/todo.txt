STOCK AI (TILL) — FRONTEND BUILD PLAN (todo.txt)
================================================================================
Derived from /server (Next.js 14 backend: stock-ai-backend) — a WhatsApp,
Voice, and Inventory/Sales Manager for Rwandan retail shops (dukas).


1. DOMAIN & BACKEND CONTEXT SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Users: Shop Owners (owner) and Workers (worker) managing daily retail
  stock & sales in Rwanda (currency: RWF, phone format: 2507XXXXXXXX / 07XXXXXXXX).
- Languages: Bilingual Kinyarwanda + English (Umuceri, Isukari, Icyayi,
  Inyungu = Profit, Gurisha = Sell, Mfite = Have/Add, Ishyura = Pay).
- Data Models (server/lib/store.ts & server/supabase/schema.sql):
  * Shop: id, phone, name, village (Nyabugogo, Kicukiro, etc.), plan
    (free | daily | monthly), credits, created_at
  * User: id, shop_id, phone, role (owner | worker), name
  * Product: id, shop_id, name, name_kv, buying_price, selling_price, quantity,
    unit (piece | kg | loaf | slice | cup), parent_id, low_stock_threshold, village
  * Sale: id, shop_id, product_id, quantity_sold, selling_price_at_time,
    buying_price_at_time, profit, sold_by (owner | worker), created_at
  * DailyReport: id, shop_id, date, total_sales, total_profit,
    total_items_sold, stock_value
  * Payment: id, shop_id, amount, type (200_daily | 5000_monthly),
    status (pending | paid), momo_ref
- Business Rules:
  * Free Plan Limit: Capped at 10 products; exceeding prompts upgrade
    (200 RWF/day or 5,000 RWF/month via MTN MoMo).
  * Parent/Child & Fractional Units: Products like Bread Loaf (loaf) can link
    to Bread Slice (slice, 0.1 unit delta) or cup (0.1 unit delta).
  * WhatsApp & Voice Commands: Natural Kinyarwanda/English commands
    (STOCK, Gurisha umuceri 2, Mfite umuceri 10, bulk 1x2, REPORT).

4. AUTHENTICATED PAGES & CORE FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] 4.1 Dashboard Overview (/dashboard)
    - KPI Cards: Total Revenue (totalSales), Net Profit (totalProfit / Inyungu),
      Active Products count (with Free Plan X / 10 meter), Remaining Stock Value.
    - Low-Stock Alerts Panel: Products at or below low_stock_threshold with
      one-click restock action.
    - Recent Sales Feed: Latest transactions showing product, quantity, profit,
      timestamp, and sold_by (owner or worker).
    - Quick Actions: Record Sale, Add Stock, Run Daily Report
      (GET /api/cron/daily-report), and Upgrade Plan.

[ ] 4.2 Inventory & Products (/products)
    - Product Catalog View: Search by English or Kinyarwanda name (name / name_kv),
      filter by unit (kg, piece, loaf, slice, cup) or low-stock status.
    - Profit Margin Column: Display buying_price, selling_price, unit margin
      (selling - buying), and current stock quantity.
    - Parent/Child Linking: Visually group fractional items (e.g., Bread Slice
      linked to Bread Loaf).
    - Add / Edit Product Modal: Form with unit selector, threshold, buying/selling
      price, and free-plan cap warning (>= 10 products triggers MoMo upgrade).
    - AI Image-to-Products Scanner: Upload/trigger shelf photo scan via
      POST /api/products/from-image, review detected items with estimated_price
      and confidence, and import selected items into stock.
    - Village Market Price Guide: Drawer/tab fetching GET /api/catalog?village=...
      to compare shop prices against local village averages.

[ ] 4.3 Quick POS, Voice & WhatsApp Simulator (/pos)
    - Fast Point-of-Sale Grid: Tap product cards, choose quantity (including
      fractional 0.1 for slices/cups or bulk ID x Qty like 1x2), select
      sold_by (owner / worker), and complete sale with live profit preview.
    - Voice Command Console: Transcribe microphone audio in a browser Web Worker,
      then send recognized text to POST /api/voice/command for stock updates.
    - WhatsApp Command Tester: Interactive chat simulator wired to
      POST /api/whatsapp/webhook for testing text commands (STOCK,
      Gurisha umuceri 2, Mfite umuceri 10, 1x2, REPORT).

[ ] 4.4 Daily Reports & Analytics (/reports)
    - Today's Summary Card: total_sales, total_profit, total_items_sold, stock_value.
    - Daily Report History Table: Date-by-date log + 7-day profit accumulation banner.
    - Owner vs. Worker Performance: Breakdown of sales and profit by role.
    - WhatsApp Report Preview & Trigger: Preview the exact Kinyarwanda WhatsApp
      summary message and trigger GET /api/cron/daily-report.

[ ] 4.5 Team & Workers Management (/team) [Owner Only]
    - List shop users (owner and worker roles) with phone numbers.
    - Add/remove worker access and view per-worker sales attribution.

[ ] 4.6 Billing & MTN MoMo Plans (/billing)
    - Plan Comparison Cards:
      * Free Plan: Up to 10 products, 20 starter credits.
      * Daily Plan (200 RWF / day): Unlimited products, 25 credits (200_daily).
      * Monthly Plan (5,000 RWF / month): Unlimited products, 200 credits (5000_monthly).
    - MoMo Checkout Flow: Enter shop phone + MoMo reference (momo_ref), submit
      to POST /api/payments, immediately update plan badge and credits.
    - Payment History Table: Past transactions (amount, type, status, momo_ref).

[ ] 4.7 Shop & Integration Settings (/settings)
    - Edit shop name, village (Nyabugogo, Kicukiro), and phone number.
    - View WhatsApp Cloud API / Supabase connection readiness and browser model status.
    - Default language preference (Kinyarwanda / English).
